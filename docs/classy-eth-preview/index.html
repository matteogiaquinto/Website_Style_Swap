One workspace for your team and AI agents

Start writing

Classy is a shared Markdown workspace where people and agents write together in real time. Think Obsidian + Notion.

Invite teammates by email, and agents like Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor with a prompt. They all work on the same documents.

One workspace for people and agents

Simpler is better

Notes are plain Markdown files. Workspaces are organized as regular files and folders. There’s no lock-in and no special formats to learn. It’s the simplest mental model for both humans and agents.

One source of truth

Local Markdown files are great until you need to collaborate. With Classy, you keep the same simple, ubiquitous format you have on your laptop, but multiplayer and real time.

Built for agents

Agents shouldn’t be restricted to a sidebar. They do their best work when you give them the same tools you’d give a colleague. With Classy, you can invite them to a file, a folder, or a whole workspace with a single prompt.

Features

  • Collaborative real-time Markdown editing.
  • Comments, presence, and multiplayer cursors.
  • Embeds, links, backlinks, and table of contents.
  • Sharing for people and agents.
  • Scoped permissions for workspaces, folders, and notes.
  • History, attribution, and restore.
  • Search across notes and files.
  • Import from Obsidian or Markdown files.
  • Export to plain .md or HTML.

Pricing

Classy is free while in beta, with full access for every account. Here’s the general direction for launch, so there are no surprises later:

Free

For individuals: 1 workspace and a few guests.

Pro
$8/member/month

For professionals and small teams: unlimited notes, private sharing, and longer history.

Team
$14/member/month

For larger teams: unlimited workspaces and granular permissions.

Enterprise
Custom

For organizations: SSO/SCIM, custom retention, and dedicated support.

Members are paid seats. Guests and agents are scoped to what you explicitly share with them.

About

I spent years using tools like Notion.

They all fell short.

What I wanted was a simple collaborative platform built on top of Markdown.

With the rise of AI agents, it became even more obvious that something like that should exist. So I built Classy, trying to make it as convenient as Notion, and as pretty as iA Writer.
